# Appendix B: Evaluated and not adopted

Each item was judged against a rule written before the result, or found impossible to evaluate. Evidence comes from 2025 data only. The competition-specific post-processing rules are in appendix A.

| Item | What it is | Why not adopted |
|---|---|---|
| Nested stacking with full refit | Three procedural changes to the baseline: nested out-of-fold computation of the extra stacked feature of ensemble B, a full refit of the LIRF sub-model, and ensemble weights fitted on non-extreme rows | Its own pre-registered gate was not met. In pooled two-fold non-extreme MSE the change was ≈ −1,770 ± 1,350 (day-block SE), while the gate required an improvement of about 530 and more than 2 SE; the refit alone gave ≈ −300 ± 700. |
| Removing the month feature | Drop the calendar month, because the test months are outside the training year | Our CV cannot evaluate it: the month is constant inside each held-out fold. Changing the baseline requires CV evidence that the change is not worse, so the feature stays. |
| EUROCONTROL monthly shift | Shift each airport × month cell towards EUROCONTROL's published monthly taxi-out time (reference plus additional time), `p' = p + α (T − P̄)`, with α chosen from {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1} inside each fold | Forward validation on 2025 (test months August to December, 5 folds) selected α = 0 in every fold, so the gate (≥ 2 SE and 5/5 folds) fails. In a 12-fold leave-one-month-out check (report only) no fold improved (0/12). After conversion to the challenge's label definition, the published monthly means vary more from month to month than our own monthly bias at most airports. Script: `evaluation/eurocontrol_shift/eval_shift.py`. |
| Grid-cell fallback for the residual encoding | When a test row's first-observation grid cell is unseen in training, use neighbouring cells instead of the airport mean (prediction side only) | Best result +0.064 ± 0.019 pp against a gate of at least +0.3 pp and more than 2 SE. The submission chain reads the residual outputs with this fallback off; the same trained models write both outputs, so nothing was retrained. |
| Switching February and June to the prediction without ADS-B | Use the baseline plus tabular term only in months without same-month ADS-B evidence in training | The switch rule (switch only if the ADS-B path is worse by at least 2 SE in 5/5 folds) did not trigger in forward validation on 2025: February −13.71 ± 1.65 s RMSE in favour of the ADS-B path (5/5 folds), June −4.49 ± 5.63 s (4/5). Scripts: `evaluation/forward_validation.py`, `evaluation/tabular_term_holdout.py`. |
